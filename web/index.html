Long runs to rooftop units and parking-lot loads fail quietly: the equipment gets less voltage than its nameplate wants. The Canadian Electrical Code guideline (Rule 8-102) is about 3% on a branch circuit and 5% in total. VD = 2 x K x I x L / A for single-phase, 1.732 in place of 2 for three-phase, with K = 12.9 for copper and 21.2 for aluminum at 75°C.
